Randall Hunt

Randall grew up in the inner-city streets of Compton, California where he taught himself to play golf hitting balls in a dirt field. While playing on a full ride golf scholarship at Pepperdine University, Randall endured a car accident that took the use of his right arm. After 3 failed surgeries Randall was told he would never use his right arm again. Randall set out to find new ways to reconnect brain to muscle tissue and invented proprietary techniques to get his arm to work again. Knowing what it’s like to suffer from poor health, Randall has dedicated his entire professional career to helping others reach the Apex of their body’s health.

Randall’s innovations and wellness companies have been used by some of the world’s most prominent organizations like the United States Olympic Training Center, Texas Health Resources Hospitals, and the #1 longevity medicine company in the world. Randall’s business acumen led to him being appointed to the Greater Orlando Aviation Authority board by the Governor of Florida to oversee 30,000 employees and $4 billion in assets. Randall is also a Florida Council of 100 member, an organization made up of the 100 top CEOs in the state of Florida.
