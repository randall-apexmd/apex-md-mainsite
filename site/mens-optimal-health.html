Frequently asked questions

What is the biggest difference between the Optimization Jumpstart and the Optimization Membership?+

The Jumpstart is a one-time evaluation: advanced bloodwork, a 1:1 physician consult and a plan you take from there. Nothing is required afterward. 

The Membership is ongoing care and starts with a more extensive bloodwork panel. It includes everything in the Jumpstart plus a 90-day personalized optimization plan, continuing 1:1 physician guidance, quarterly labs with protocol adjustments, personalized therapies and nutrition guidance, and priority support from the Apex MD care team.

Put simply: the Jumpstart tells you what is happening. The Membership keeps a physician working on it with you.

What is the difference between the Men’s Optimal Health Program and the Men’s Testosterone Program?+

The Men’s Testosterone Program focuses on men’s hormones, where the Men’s Optimal Health bloodwork panel has a more extensive dive into metabolic, lipid and optimal health biomarkers.

How does the blood lab process work?+

You can draw at a local lab or at home — whichever fits your week.

In-lab. After purchase you receive a secure lab order by email. Visit any LabCorp or Quest location, many of which sit inside Walgreens or CVS, and show the form on your phone. Results upload automatically to your Apex MD portal and our team reaches out to schedule your consult.

At home. We ship a complete kit to your door. The test takes under 15 minutes and includes a prepaid FedEx return label. Drop it off, and your results are added securely to your portal.

What is included in my program?+

A comprehensive metabolic and hormone panel; a medical review and health optimization plan from a licensed provider; a registered dietitian consult with up to a year of ongoing coaching if approved by insurance, accepted by over 98% of plans; a customized fitness outline with full Apex MD app access; and the Apex MD health portal for your health score, lab results and follow-up records.

There are no required ongoing fees after your initial consultation. If your results indicate you would benefit from testosterone optimization, vitamin therapy or supplement support, you can move forward at your own pace. Vitamins and supplements start at $19 per month; testosterone therapy is $199 per month. No hidden costs.
