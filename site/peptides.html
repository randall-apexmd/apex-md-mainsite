FAQs

Your questions, answered

What is NAD+?

Nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide is a form of vitamin B3 (niacin). It is an essential coenzyme found in the mitochondria — the “energy powerhouses” — of all living cells. It is heavily involved in DNA repair and cellular remodeling. It has been known to “turn off” genes that accelerate degenerative aging, and is essential for maintaining the neurotransmitter levels the brain needs to function properly. It is key in cellular metabolism. Heart, brain and muscle cells contain the highest concentration of NAD+, and the more NAD+ present in cells, the more efficiently they function. As we age, NAD+ levels tend to decline, making us less able to repair cellular damage: we lose energy, appear older and have decreased brain function.

NAD+ plays a significant role in anti-aging. The molecule works rapidly to repair cells throughout the body and neurons in the brain. It serves as a catalyst in brain restoration, which assists in overcoming depression, anxiety and common mood disorders. Restoring and supplementing NAD+ may improve physical appearance, health and quality of life.

What are the benefits of NAD+?

Supplementation of NAD+ may:

  • Increase energy levels
  • Enhance mood and mental clarity
  • Reverse the visible effects of aging
  • Reduce fatigue
  • Restore muscle function and athletic performance
  • Restore brain chemistry and neurotransmitter function
  • Prevent and correct damage to DNA
  • Regulate inflammation that damages DNA
  • Improve physical appearance, health and quality of life
Are there side effects from NAD+?

The majority of individuals do not encounter any adverse effects from using NAD+. Should you experience any sensitivity after using NAD+, consider selecting a different site on your body for the next injection. As with any side effect, if symptoms continue, stop use and get in touch with our team. Side effects that can be experienced with NAD+ include headache, flushing, nausea, diarrhea, dizziness, cramping, fatigue, and redness or bruising at the injection site.

What is Sermorelin?

Sermorelin is a synthetic version of a naturally occurring substance that causes the release of growth hormone from the pituitary gland. Growth hormone is naturally produced by the pituitary gland and is necessary for growth in children.

What are the benefits of taking Sermorelin?

Sermorelin treatments have proven effective for many patients. Benefits include, but are not limited to: enhanced energy, improved mood, improved sleep, increased bone density, increased mental acuity, reduced feelings of anxiety and depression, and weight loss or improved muscle mass.

Is Sermorelin used for weight loss?

Sermorelin may help patients lose weight because growth hormone increases the production of insulin-like growth factor 1 (IGF-1). Higher levels of IGF-1 raise metabolism, which aids weight loss.

What are the side effects of Sermorelin?

Sermorelin injections can cause common side effects at the injection site, such as irritation, itching, sensitivity, swelling, pain and redness. Less common side effects include difficulty swallowing, dizziness, flushing of the face or neck, headache, nausea, rash, sleepiness, taste changes and restlessness. These usually last only a short time and need no action. If side effects worsen after an injection, hold the medication and consult your medical provider.

If you develop signs of a severe allergic reaction, such as swelling of the lips, tongue or face or difficulty breathing, call 911 and seek emergency care immediately.

What is MIC B12?

MIC B12 is an injectable supplement that combines vitamin B12 with three lipotropic compounds: methionine, inositol and choline. Together they support metabolism, liver function and energy levels.

What is MIC B12 used for?

MIC B12 is primarily used to support fat metabolism, enhance energy levels and promote liver detoxification. It’s often included in medical weight loss plans to help optimize the body’s ability to burn fat and maintain energy during calorie-restricted diets.

Who should not take MIC B12?

MIC B12 may not be suitable for people with certain health conditions, including liver or kidney disease, allergies to cobalamin (B12) or any of the MIC components, or certain metabolic disorders. Pregnant or breastfeeding women should consult a healthcare provider before use.

Are there any side effects of MIC B12?

Side effects are generally mild but can include redness or irritation at the injection site, mild digestive upset, or increased urination. Rarely, individuals may experience allergic reactions. Always consult a healthcare provider if you notice unusual symptoms.
