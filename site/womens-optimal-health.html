The power of estrogen

Estrogen is not
a reproductive
hormone. It is a
whole-body one.

Estrogen receptors sit in your brain, bone, blood vessels, gut and skin. When estrogen falls, those tissues lose a signal they have depended on for decades — which is why the symptoms of hormone decline rarely stay in one place.

2 in 3

people living with Alzheimer’s disease are women.

Living longer than men does not fully account for the gap. Researchers are actively investigating the role of the sharp estrogen loss at menopause in that difference — which is why brain health belongs in a hormone conversation, not after it.

Alzheimer’s Association Facts & Figures
Brain

Estrogen supports glucose use and synaptic function in the brain. Word-finding trouble and brain fog in midlife are hormonal signals worth measuring, not personality changes to accept.

Bone

Bone loss accelerates sharply in the years around menopause. Density is far easier to protect than to rebuild, which makes the timing of this conversation the point.

Heart

Cardiovascular risk in women rises after menopause as lipids and vascular flexibility shift. Heart disease remains the leading cause of death in women — and it is trackable on a panel.

Metabolism

Falling estrogen shifts fat storage toward the abdomen and worsens insulin sensitivity. This is the mechanism behind weight that will not move on the same diet that used to work.

volume problem

02 Insulin resistance adds volume
03 Collagen and circulation thin out

Fat sits in chambers separated by fibrous bands that anchor skin to the muscle beneath. When those chambers hold more fat than the bands allow room for, the fat pushes upward between them and the surface dimples.

That is why cellulite tracks with hormone and metabolic status, not willpower. Reduce the fat volume in those chambers and improve the tissue around them, and the surface changes with it.

The Optimization Jumpstart is a one-time purchase: the full metabolic and hormone panel, a consult with an Apex MD physician, and supplement, peptide and hormone guidance so you leave with answers and clear next steps. It ends there, with no ongoing commitment.

The Optimization Membership starts with a more extensive bloodwork panel and consult, then carries the plan forward — a 90-day optimization plan, prescribed therapies and personalized vitamins, unlimited medical support, and follow-up labs with protocol adjustments as your numbers change.

What is the difference between the Women’s Optimal Health Program and the Hormone Therapy Program?+

The Hormone Therapy Program focuses on hormones alone, where the Women’s Optimal Health bloodwork panel takes a far more extensive dive into metabolic, thyroid, lipid and optimal health biomarkers.

How does the blood lab process work?+

You can draw at a local lab or at home — whichever fits your week.

In-lab. After purchase you receive a secure lab order by email. Visit any LabCorp or Quest location, many of which sit inside Walgreens or CVS, and show the form on your phone. Results upload automatically to your Apex MD portal and our team reaches out to schedule your consult.

At home. We ship a complete kit to your door. The test takes under 15 minutes and includes a prepaid FedEx return label. Drop it off, and your results are added securely to your portal.
