What microdosing is

A fraction of the dose, aimed at a different outcome

Full-dose GLP-1s are built for weight loss. Microdosing uses the same medications at a much lower dose, combined with targeted additives, to work on inflammation, energy, cognition and metabolic health while you hold your muscle.

What goes in the mix

The additives, and what each one is for

Your personalized mix can include any of these. Open one to see why it’s used alongside a GLP-1.

NAD+
  • Enhances cellular energy production, which can counteract the fatigue and low energy often experienced on GLP-1 medications.
  • Supports mitochondrial function and fat metabolism, potentially amplifying the weight-management effects of GLP-1 agonists.
  • Provides anti-aging and longevity benefits by promoting cellular repair and reducing oxidative stress.
  • May mitigate side effects such as muscle loss or metabolic slowdown by aiding detoxification and preserving vitality.
Vitamin B-12
  • Prevents or treats deficiency caused by reduced food intake or altered absorption, supporting energy and red blood cell formation.
  • Enhances fat metabolism and energy production, potentially boosting the efficacy of GLP-1 agonists.
  • Supports nervous system health and may reduce fatigue during GLP-1 therapy.
  • Aids in preserving lean muscle mass and motivation.
Vitamin B-6
  • Reduces nausea, a frequent GLP-1 side effect, improving tolerance and adherence.
  • Supports metabolism and energy production, potentially enhancing blood sugar control and weight loss.
  • Aids neurotransmitter synthesis, which may help manage mood and stress during treatment.
  • May improve sleep quality and cognitive function.
Magnesium
  • Supports blood sugar control and insulin sensitivity, enhancing GLP-1’s glycemic effects.
  • May alleviate constipation, a common GLP-1 side effect, by promoting bowel regularity.
  • Aids muscle and nerve function, reducing cramps or fatigue during therapy.
  • Helps manage stress and improve sleep.
Choline
  • Aids fat transport and metabolism, supporting liver health and potentially preventing fat buildup.
  • Essential for cell structure and messaging, which may help maintain metabolic function during reduced calorie intake.
  • May improve blood sugar regulation alongside GLP-1 agonists.
  • Supports DNA synthesis and nervous system health, helping mitigate nutrient deficiencies from appetite suppression.
Inositol
  • Supports fat metabolism and mood balance, potentially aiding GLP-1 effects and reducing emotional eating.
  • May improve insulin sensitivity, complementing glucose regulation.
  • Helps with PCOS-related symptoms.
  • Supports liver function and cellular health during reduced calorie intake.
Methionine
  • Supports fat breakdown and liver detoxification as part of lipotropic blends.
  • Aids in preventing excess fat buildup in the liver during rapid weight loss.
  • Contributes to energy production and may help manage fatigue.
  • Essential for protein synthesis, supporting muscle maintenance during calorie reduction.
Glycine
  • May support metabolic health and reduce inflammation.
  • Aids detoxification and liver function during fat loss.
  • Promotes better sleep and cognitive function, potentially mitigating fatigue.
  • Supports gut health and regularity.
L-theanine
  • Used in the evening formulation to support calm and deeper sleep.
  • Paired with magnesium to take you further into REM sleep.
L-carnitine
  • Supports the transport of fatty acids for energy production.
  • Used in Apex MD’s full-dose GLP-1 blends alongside NAD+ and B-12.

These are the roles each additive plays in the formulation. They are not a claim that any one ingredient treats a condition on its own, and your mix is set by your provider.
